The concept of gentrification is associated with so many other words these days — “[graffiti](https://vm.tiktok.com/ZMRFNMTec/),” “[rock music](https://twitter.com/kanaruaizawa16/status/1428459593781366793),” “[font](https://twitter.com/412goose/status/1428306759651897352),” “[thrifting](https://vm.tiktok.com/ZMRFNufos/)” — that it bears little similarity to its original definition. In 1964, sociologist Ruth Glass coined the term “gentrification”. As Steven Thomson [explained for Curbed](https://archive.curbed.com/2014/11/5/10028070/tracing-the-history-of-a-word-as-gentrification-turns-50), the American (1)\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ and urban design website, Glass was describing a “class (2)\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ … by adapting the British-ism ‘gentry’” to describe the process of “(3)\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ liberal arts intellectuals” moving into her primarily working-class London neighborhood.

The term flew across the Atlantic and made its home in the United States, where similar trends would begin to be (4)\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ over the last few decades of the 20th century. Google Books [data](https://books.google.com/ngrams/graph?content=gentrification&year_start=1800&year_end=2019&corpus=26&smoothing=3&direct_url=t1%3B%2Cgentrification%3B%2Cc0) shows the term “gentrification” didn’t really take off in the US until the late ’90s and has been steadily growing in use, ever since.

There isn’t an agreed-upon empirical definition of gentrification among scholars, which makes it difficult, even (5)\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to talk about it with any certainty. But talk we do: From [Indianapolis](https://www.indystar.com/story/news/local/2021/08/05/indianapolis-martindale-brightwood-neighborhood-gentrification-housing-real-estate/5400399001/) to [Austin](https://www.insider.com/photos-east-austin-cooleast-neighborhood-changed-gentrification-holly-2019-11#:~:text=Holly%20is%20in%20the%20accelerated,pricing%20out%20low%2Dincome%20residents.), on a [presidential debate stage](https://www.ourhomes-ourvotes.org/post/joe-biden-gentrification-foe-has-a-housing-plan) and on a [panel on bike lanes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZnH6zf3CLP8), and of course, on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/humancomedian/status/877652828000575488?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw%7Ctwcamp%5Etweetembed%7Ctwterm%5E877652828000575488%7Ctwgr%5E%7Ctwcon%5Es1_&ref_url=https%3A%2F%2Fmashable.com%2Farticle%2Fbehind-tweet-gentrification-ebt-humancomedian).
