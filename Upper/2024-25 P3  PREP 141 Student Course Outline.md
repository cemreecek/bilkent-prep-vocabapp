**Approach to Reading at Upper-Intermediate Level**

At Upper-intermediate level, we introduce the learner to discursive/argumentative texts related to topics that are accessible to them and that present a greater level of conceptual difficulty. Learners will be expected to already have the language, the reading skills, and knowledge of text structure to help them start dealing with such texts. At this level they will be expected to construct meaning from texts in which arguments are put forward and defended or supported through ideas (as well as through details and examples). It is expected that learners will develop their language more on an academic basis (i.e. through academic vocabulary) rather than on a general English basis alone. By the end of the level, learners should be able to construct meaning from discursive as well as narrative, descriptive and informative texts, even when they are less-structured.

**Approach to Listening at Upper-Intermediate Level**

At Upper-intermediate level, we introduce the learner to well-structured, straight-forward lectures on topics that are accessible to them and that present a greater level of conceptual difficulty. Learners will be expected to already have the language, listening skills, and knowledge of text organization to help them start dealing with lectures. At this level they will be expected to construct meaning from lectures in which arguments are put forward and defended/ supported through ideas (as well as through details and examples). This will involve understanding signposting and use of complex discourse markers and linkers. Note-taking skills will also be further developed and there will be less guidance (in the form of headings) provided to the students. It is expected that learners will develop their language more on an academic basis rather than on a general English basis alone. They will also continue to develop their listening skills through detailed narratives, descriptions and dialogues. By the end of the level, learners should be able to construct meaning from discursive as well as narrative, descriptive and informative texts.

**Approach to Writing at Upper-Intermediate Level**

At Upper-intermediate level, the focus is on putting forward an argument in writing and supporting it in a meaningful way through the format of a well-structured essay. Learners will write opinion essays related to concrete and abstract topics that are accessible to them. They will be expected to already have a knowledge of essay structure to help them start writing such essays. At this level, learners will be expected to produce elaborate thesis statements and topic sentences by putting forward arguments and supporting them through details and examples. This will involve a focus on thesis statements topic sentences, paragraphing and essay structure. It is expected that learners will further develop their language more on an academic basis. They will further develop their writing skills through letters, emails, journals and creative writing. By the end of the level, learners should be able to construct meaning producing narrative, descriptive and informative texts that show problem/solution, cause/effect relationships with an awareness of style and register.

**Approach to Speaking at Upper-Intermediate Level**

At Upper-intermediate level, the focus is on more complex conversations, discussions and well-structured oral presentations which are more discursive/argumentative and interactive in nature. Learners will be expected to have the language and speaking skills to be able to talk about more complex ideas. At this level, they will be expected to develop an argument and defend or support it through ideas (as well as through details and examples), and structure their presentations using relevant discourse markers, fillers, back-channeling and signposting. There will also be a focus on turn-taking skills and specified conversation gambits. It is expected that learners will develop their language more on an academic basis rather than on a general English basis alone. By the end of the level, learners should be able to take part in discussions and conversations on familiar topics with less preparation, and give well-structured oral presentations.
