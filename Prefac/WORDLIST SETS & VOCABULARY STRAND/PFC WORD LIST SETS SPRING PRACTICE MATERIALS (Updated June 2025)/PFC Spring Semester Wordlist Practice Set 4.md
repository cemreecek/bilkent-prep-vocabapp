1. A minimum B minimal

|  |
| --- |
| **Minimum** should be used when you want to quantify the smallest amount of something, like a countable noun or giving a number. **Minimal** should be used when you want to qualify something, like non-countable nouns or when you say something is barely adequate. |

1. A comprehensive B comprehensible

|  |
| --- |
| **Comprehensive:** including all the necessary facts, details, or problems that need to be dealt with  e.g. We offer our customers a comprehensive range of financial products.  ► Do not confuse with **comprehensible** (=able to be understood).  e.g. His report was barely comprehensible. |

1. A deficient B deficit

|  |
| --- |
| **Deficient:** not having enough of stg or not good enough  **Deficit:** the difference between the amount of something that you have and the higher amount that you need |

1. A simultaneous B spontaneous

|  |
| --- |
| Something that is **spontaneous** has not been planned or organized, but happens by itself, or because you suddenly feel you want to do it.  Things that are **simultaneous** happen at exactly the same time. |

1. A figurative B literal

|  |
| --- |
| The **literal** meaning of a word or expression is its basic or original meaning e.g. literal meaning/sense/interpretation etc  A **figurative** word or phrase is used in a different way from its usual meaning, to give you a particular idea or picture in your mind. |
