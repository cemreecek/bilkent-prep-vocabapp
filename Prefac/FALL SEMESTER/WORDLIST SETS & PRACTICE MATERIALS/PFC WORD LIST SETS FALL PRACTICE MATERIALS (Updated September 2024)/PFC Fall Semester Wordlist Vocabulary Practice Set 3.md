PART 1

Match the following verbs with their definitions. 



eliminate

to make it difficult for something to develop or succeed 

adjust

to completely get rid of something that is unnecessary or unwanted

hinder

to shake slightly in a way that you cannot control, especially because you are upset or frightened

insist

to gradually become familiar with a new situation

tremble

to behave in the way that most other people in your group or society behave

conform

to say firmly and often that something is true, especially when other people think it may not be true



Match the following nouns with their definitions. 



famine

a particular attitude or way of thinking, especially one that you think is wrong or stupid

mentality

facts, information, documents etc that prove something is true

consensus

a very high level of intelligence, mental skill, or ability, which only a few people have

proof

the ability to do something well

genius

a situation in which a large number of people have little or no food for a long time and many people die

competence

an opinion that everyone in a group agrees with or accepts



Match the following adjectives with their definitions. 



loose

very large, solid, and heavy

feasible

used to describe someone or something that has been given a name that you think is wrong

massive

almost a particular thing or quality; made, done, seen etc on the internet or on a computer, rather than in the real world

so-called

not attached or fastened to anything else

uneasy

stg that is possible, reasonable, or likely

virtual

slightly worried or uncomfortable about a particular situation

grand

impressive in size, appearance, or style
